How to Export Your Contacts

Export as CSV or VCF (vCard) from your contacts provider.

Google Contacts

  1. Go to contacts.google.com
  2. Click Export in the left sidebar
  3. Choose Google CSV format
  4. Click Export to download

📖 Official Google guide →

Apple Contacts (Mac)

  1. Open the Contacts app
  2. Select all contacts with ⌘A
  3. Go to File → Export → Export vCard…
  4. Save the .vcf file

📖 Official Apple guide →

Apple (iPhone / iPad)

  1. Open the Contacts app
  2. Tap "Lists" in the top-left corner
  3. Touch and hold "All Contacts" (or a group)
  4. Tap "Export" → "Save to Files"

📖 Official Apple guide →

Outlook & Others

Most providers support CSV or vCard export:

  1. Outlook: Visit Outlook People → Manage → Export
  2. Yahoo, Samsung, etc.: Check your provider's settings for export options
