# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring birthday patterns across a contact collection, including on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Import contacts and explore birthdays through timeline, calendar, upcoming, statistics, shared birthdays, and missing-data views.

## Positioning

Contacts are parsed and explored locally in the browser; the product offers several linked ways to inspect the same birthday data.

## Operating Context

Users bring CSV or vCard exports, or open a fictional demo dataset. They search and filter contacts by name, tag, and month.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing views, import formats, filters, settings, and private browser-only processing. The app is a static single-file HTML/CSS/JavaScript site hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md documents the features and import workflows. demo-contacts.csv contains fictional sample contacts. Existing screenshots in screenshots/ document the current UI.

## Product Principles

- Make exploration across views easy to discover.
- Keep contact information readable and reachable on small screens.
- Process contacts locally and communicate that clearly.
